# Third-party brand marks

These small marks are bundled locally so the demo does not make runtime logo
requests or leak portfolio symbols to an image service.

- Vector company marks were downloaded from Simple Icons.
- Raster marks are public favicons returned for each company's official domain.
- All company and brokerage names and marks remain trademarks of their
  respective owners. Their presence identifies simulated holdings and account
  connections; it does not imply affiliation or endorsement.

When adding a symbol, update `src/data/brandLogos.ts` and include a local asset
with a deterministic ticker fallback.
